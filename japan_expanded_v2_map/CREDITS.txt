Japan historical province and district boundaries
================================================

The map builder uses transformed and rasterized boundary data from:

  "Old Provinces / Old Districts Dataset" by the Center for Open Data in
  the Humanities (CODH), processed from the Bakumatsu-Meiji topographic-map
  boundary data created by the National Institutes for the Humanities.

  DOI: https://doi.org/10.20676/00000454
  Dataset: https://geoshape.ex.nii.ac.jp/kg/

Required Japanese attribution:

  『旧国・旧郡データセット』（CODH作成）「幕末明治地勢地図境界データ」
  （人間文化研究機構作成）を加工 doi:10.20676/00000454

The source dataset is published under CC BY-NC. The generated EU4 raster is
a simplified, transformed, non-commercial derivative. EU4 coastlines remain
those of Europa Universalis IV 1.37.5; no source polygon is copied verbatim to
the game map.
